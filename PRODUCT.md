# Product

## Register

product

## Users

University students building long-term revision knowledge bases.

## Product Purpose

Knowledge Review Agent turns university course material into a searchable, durable knowledge base. Users study consolidated technical and professional concepts through readable concept pages, provenance, and graph relationships.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, scholarly.

## Anti-references

Avoid cluttered dashboards, neon colors, unexplained metrics, decorative UI, and presentation polish that obscures the knowledge itself.

## Design Principles

- Put learning flow before pipeline metadata.
- Make relationships and provenance understandable without requiring technical recall.
- Prefer restrained, high-contrast presentation over decoration.
- Keep dense information scannable through hierarchy and progressive disclosure.
- Treat the graph as a navigation aid, not the primary content surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, readable contrast, visible focus states, color-independent meaning, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion preferences.
