## Definition
Margin of Error is the half-width that quantifies sampling uncertainty around a point estimate, commonly computed as a critical value multiplied by a standard error. It is used in Survey Estimation. It is commonly considered alongside Confidence Interval, Point Estimation.

## Why it Matters
It matters because it makes Survey Estimation measurable, enabling explicit comparisons and decisions.
