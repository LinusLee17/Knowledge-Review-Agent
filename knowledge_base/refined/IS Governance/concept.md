## Definition
IS Governance is a framework in Digital Transformation focused on Governance. It helps people coordinate action, manage change, and make better decisions in organisational settings.

## Why it Matters
IS Governance matters because it supports Accountability, Control, Decision Rights with an explicit concept and evidence trail.

## Core Intuition
Think of IS Governance as a repeatable way to align people, decisions, and execution so the team moves toward Governance with less friction.

## How it Works

2. Align actions, communication, and responsibilities around that goal.
3. Review the outcome, surface resistance or gaps, and adjust the approach.
