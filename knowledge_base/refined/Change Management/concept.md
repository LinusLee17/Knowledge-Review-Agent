## Definition
Change Management is a practice in Leadership focused on Organizational Change. It helps people coordinate action, manage change, and make better decisions in organisational settings.

## Why it Matters
Change Management matters because it supports Adoption Planning, Culture Change, Transformation Programs with an explicit concept and evidence trail.

## Core Intuition
Think of Change Management as a repeatable way to align people, decisions, and execution so the team moves toward Organizational Change with less friction.

## How it Works
1. Clarify the goal and the people affected by Change Management.
2. Align actions, communication, and responsibilities around that goal.
3. Review the outcome, surface resistance or gaps, and adjust the approach.

## Common Mistakes
- Treating Change Management as only a slogan instead of a repeatable practice.
- Ignoring the people, context, or constraints around Change Management.
- Assuming Change Management works without communication or follow-through.
