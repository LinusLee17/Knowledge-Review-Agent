## Definition
Business Continuity Management is a framework in Business focused on Resilience. It helps people coordinate action, manage change, and make better decisions in organisational settings.

## Why it Matters
Business Continuity Management matters because it supports Disruption Planning, Recovery, Service Resilience with an explicit concept and evidence trail.

## Core Intuition
Think of Business Continuity Management as a repeatable way to align people, decisions, and execution so the team moves toward Resilience with less friction.

## How it Works
1. Clarify the goal and the people affected by Business Continuity Management.
2. Align actions, communication, and responsibilities around that goal.
3. Review the outcome, surface resistance or gaps, and adjust the approach.

## Key Components
- Goal

## Common Mistakes
- Treating Business Continuity Management as only a slogan instead of a repeatable practice.
- Ignoring the people, context, or constraints around Business Continuity Management.
- Assuming Business Continuity Management works without communication or follow-through.
