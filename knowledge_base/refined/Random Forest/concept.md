## Definition
Random Forest is an ensemble that combines predictions from many randomized decision trees to reduce variance and improve generalization. It is used in Customer Analytics, Fraud Detection, Healthcare Prediction. It is commonly considered alongside Decision Trees, Gradient Boosting, XGBoost.

## Why it Matters
It matters because it provides a concrete way to reason about Customer Analytics, Fraud Detection, Healthcare Prediction and choose an appropriate next action.
