## Definition
Project Management is a workplace skill in Project Management focused on Project Delivery. It supports everyday execution, collaboration, and communication.

## Why it Matters
Project Management matters because it supports Delivery Planning, Execution, Scheduling with an explicit concept and evidence trail.

## Core Intuition
Think of Project Management as a repeatable way to align people, decisions, and execution so the team moves toward Project Delivery with less friction.

## How it Works
1. Clarify the goal and the people affected by Project Management.
2. Align actions, communication, and responsibilities around that goal.
3. Review the outcome, surface resistance or gaps, and adjust the approach.
