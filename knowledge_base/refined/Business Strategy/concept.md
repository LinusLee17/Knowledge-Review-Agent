## Definition
Business Strategy is a framework in Business focused on Strategy Alignment. It helps people coordinate action, manage change, and make better decisions in organisational settings.

## Why it Matters
Business Strategy matters because it supports Competitive Advantage, Resource Allocation, Strategic Alignment with an explicit concept and evidence trail.

## Core Intuition
Think of Business Strategy as a repeatable way to align people, decisions, and execution so the team moves toward Strategy Alignment with less friction.

## How it Works
1. Clarify the goal and the people affected by Business Strategy.
2. Align actions, communication, and responsibilities around that goal.
3. Review the outcome, surface resistance or gaps, and adjust the approach.
