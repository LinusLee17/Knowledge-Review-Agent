## Definition
Communication is a workplace skill in Communication focused on Leadership Communication. It supports everyday execution, collaboration, and communication.

## Why it Matters
Communication matters because it supports Presentation, Stakeholder Alignment, Team Collaboration with an explicit concept and evidence trail.

## Core Intuition
Think of Communication as a repeatable way to align people, decisions, and execution so the team moves toward Leadership Communication with less friction.

## How it Works
1. Clarify the goal and the people affected by Communication.
2. Align actions, communication, and responsibilities around that goal.
3. Review the outcome, surface resistance or gaps, and adjust the approach.
