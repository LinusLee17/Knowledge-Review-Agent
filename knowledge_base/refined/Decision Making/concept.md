## Definition
Decision Making is a workplace skill in Business focused on Decision Making. It supports everyday execution, collaboration, and communication.

## Why it Matters
Decision Making matters because it supports Planning, Priority Setting, Trade-off Analysis with an explicit concept and evidence trail.

## Core Intuition
Think of Decision Making as a repeatable way to align people, decisions, and execution so the team moves toward Decision Making with less friction.

## How it Works
1. Clarify the goal and the people affected by Decision Making.
2. Align actions, communication, and responsibilities around that goal.
3. Review the outcome, surface resistance or gaps, and adjust the approach.
