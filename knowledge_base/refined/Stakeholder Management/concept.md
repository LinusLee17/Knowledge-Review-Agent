## Definition
Stakeholder Management is a practice in Leadership focused on Stakeholder Engagement. It helps people coordinate action, manage change, and make better decisions in organisational settings.

## Why it Matters
Stakeholder Management matters because it supports Change Initiatives, Cross-functional Alignment, Project Delivery with an explicit concept and evidence trail.

## Core Intuition
Think of Stakeholder Management as a repeatable way to align people, decisions, and execution so the team moves toward Stakeholder Engagement with less friction.

## How it Works
1. Clarify the goal and the people affected by Stakeholder Management.
2. Align actions, communication, and responsibilities around that goal.
3. Review the outcome, surface resistance or gaps, and adjust the approach.

## Common Mistakes
- Treating Stakeholder Management as only a slogan instead of a repeatable practice.
