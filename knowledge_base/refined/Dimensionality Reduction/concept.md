## Definition
Dimensionality Reduction is a systematic procedure for transforming inputs into an output or decision in Unsupervised Learning. It is used in Compression, Noise Reduction, Visualization. It is commonly considered alongside Feature Engineering, Model Selection, PCA.

## Why it Matters
It matters because it provides a concrete way to reason about Compression, Noise Reduction, Visualization and choose an appropriate next action.

## Core Intuition
Keep the strongest structure while replacing many noisy coordinates with fewer useful ones.
