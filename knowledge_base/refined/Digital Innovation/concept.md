## Definition
Digital Innovation is a principle in Digital Transformation focused on Innovation. It helps people coordinate action, manage change, and make better decisions in organisational settings.

## Why it Matters
Digital Innovation matters because it supports Competitive Advantage, New Product Development, Process Improvement with an explicit concept and evidence trail.

## Core Intuition
Think of Digital Innovation as a repeatable way to align people, decisions, and execution so the team moves toward Innovation with less friction.

## How it Works
1. Clarify the goal and the people affected by Digital Innovation.
2. Align actions, communication, and responsibilities around that goal.
3. Review the outcome, surface resistance or gaps, and adjust the approach.
