## Definition
Leadership is a framework in Leadership focused on Leadership Foundations. It helps people coordinate action, manage change, and make better decisions in organisational settings.

## Why it Matters
Leadership matters because it supports IT Management, Organisational Change, Team Direction with an explicit concept and evidence trail.

## Core Intuition
Think of Leadership as a repeatable way to align people, decisions, and execution so the team moves toward Leadership Foundations with less friction.

## How it Works
1. Clarify the goal and the people affected by Leadership.
2. Align actions, communication, and responsibilities around that goal.
3. Review the outcome, surface resistance or gaps, and adjust the approach.
