## Definition
Internal Validity is the degree to which an observed effect within a study can be attributed to the treatment or exposure rather than confounding, selection, measurement, or other systematic bias.

## Why it Matters
Internal Validity matters because it supports A/B Testing, Experimentation, Policy Evaluation with an explicit concept and evidence trail.

## Core Intuition
Ask whether the study itself supports the claimed cause before asking whether the result generalizes.
