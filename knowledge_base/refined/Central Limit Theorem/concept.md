## Definition
Central Limit Theorem states that, under suitable conditions, the standardized sum or sample mean approaches a normal distribution as sample size grows. It is used in Approximation, Inference.

## Why it Matters
It matters because it provides a concrete way to reason about Approximation, Inference and choose an appropriate next action.
